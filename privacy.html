How the PENI website handles information you share with us.

This notice describes the information PENI collects when you shop, contact us, or submit a review. Review this policy alongside our terms.

01 / INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE

Checkout collects your name, email, phone number, delivery address and selected items. Contact forms collect the details you enter. Reviews collect your name, rating and written feedback.

02 / HOW IT IS USED

We use customer information to process orders, assist with delivery, answer enquiries, review submitted feedback and support the operation of the store.

03 / PAYMENTS

Payments are handled by Paystack. PENI does not collect your card number or card security code through the website. The payment provider processes payments under its own terms and privacy practices.

04 / LOCAL DEVICE STORAGE

The website stores your shopping bag in your browser so it remains available when you return. Administrators authenticate through Firebase Authentication; order and support information is stored on Firebase services when configured.

05 / STORAGE & SECURITY

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet service is completely risk-free. Customer information is accessible only to authorized personnel with a legitimate business purpose.

06 / YOUR QUESTIONS

For questions about data held by PENI or to request correction or deletion where applicable, contact us through the website.

This operational policy should be reviewed and completed with the business owner’s actual practices and applicable legal requirements before production launch.
