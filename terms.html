What to know when shopping with PENI.

Please read these terms before placing an order with PENI.

01 / PRODUCTS & AVAILABILITY

Product details and prices are shown on each listing. Availability is subject to confirmation, and we may need to contact you if a selected product is unavailable.

02 / PRICING & PAYMENT

Prices are displayed in Nigerian Naira (NGN). The checkout summary shows the selected delivery option and total amount before you proceed. Orders are not considered paid until payment is verified by our payment provider.

03 / DELIVERY

Delivery timing and coverage vary by destination and product availability. Delivery options presented at checkout are subject to confirmation. Express handling does not guarantee a fixed delivery time.

04 / RETURNS & EXCHANGES

Contact PENI promptly if an order arrives incorrect or damaged. Whether an item is eligible for a return or exchange depends on its condition, applicable law and the circumstances of the order. We will provide next steps after reviewing the request.

05 / PRODUCT IMAGES & REPRESENTATION

The current collection uses designed placeholders rather than photographs. Confirm product features with PENI before ordering if you require additional details.

06 / CONTACT

Questions about an order or these terms can be sent through our contact page.

Before launching, confirm full business identity, delivery geography, refund deadlines and legal terms with the business owner.
